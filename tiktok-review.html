Workflow and product story

This is the kind of public page TikTok expects to see during app review: a real website that explains what the app does, how the TikTok features work, and where the legal documents live.

Login Kit

Users can connect their TikTok account through OAuth so the app can identify the creator, confirm the destination account, and continue only after the user consents.

Content Posting API

The user selects a video, edits the caption and privacy options, then manually presses publish. The app uploads the file and polls status until the post is complete.

Multi-brand dashboard

Multipost organizes work by brand workspace so teams can publish to different accounts without repeated login switching or accidental cross-posting.
