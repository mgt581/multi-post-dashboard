How MultiPost works

See the steps and subscription terms before choosing a plan. This guide is available before you subscribe.

Start with one workspace

  1. Create an account or sign in. Verify your email if prompted.
  2. Review the subscription in Profile. On Android, Google Play shows available monthly and yearly plans and any trial you are eligible for.
  3. Create a workspace for a brand or client. Pro is intended to include one workspace. Paid extra workspaces are not available yet.
  4. Open the workspace and connect a YouTube account, Facebook Page or TikTok account. Grant each provider the required permissions.
  5. Open the post studio to enter a description or image, generate platform SEO, review the text and publish using an available platform flow. You can edit copy yourself. TikTok may ask you to finish a draft inside TikTok.

Scheduled posting is not available in this version. Instagram publishing is not available in this version.

Plans, trial and renewal

For UK customers the proposed MultiPost Pro prices are £9.99 per month or £79.99 per year. Google Play displays the current price in your local currency and the exact first payment date before you confirm. Your plan renews automatically until you cancel it.

A seven-day free-trial offer is planned for eligible first-time subscribers on either billing period. Google Play decides eligibility. The app only shows a trial option when Play returns that offer for your account. If there is no eligible offer, the paid base plan is shown.

Pro is planned to include 150 AI credits and 150 successful platform publications each month, including when billed yearly. These allowances and extra credit purchases are not available yet. Review the final plan terms in the app before subscribing.

To cancel or manage a Play subscription, open Profile → Manage in Google Play. Cancelling stops renewal; access can continue until the verified expiry. Existing web subscribers can use Profile → Manage Billing for the Stripe portal. Restore purchases on Android to recheck a Play purchase for the signed-in MultiPost account.

Extra workspaces

One workspace is intended to be included with Pro. Extra workspaces are proposed at £4.99 each per month or £39.99 each per year in the UK. Recurring multiple-workspace purchases and matching entitlements are still under design; there is no working add-on purchase button yet.

If something does not work

  • If workspaces do not load, use Retry on the dashboard. Sign in again if your session expired; check your network connection if the server is unavailable.
  • If a purchase is missing, check that you are using the original Google Play account and the same MultiPost sign-in, then use Restore purchases. A purchase screen alone does not activate access; the server must verify it.
  • If a social account fails to connect, open that workspace and reconnect it. Provider permissions or account restrictions may need attention.
  • If a post fails, read the platform-specific error and retry once your connection is restored. Failed publishing attempts should not be treated as a successful post.

Account and data deletion

Profile can remove your sign-in, but that action does not cancel a Play or Stripe subscription or erase your saved workspaces and social connections. Cancel billing separately, then email info@multipostapp.co.uk to request full account and data deletion.

Subscription purchases and extra workspace upgrades will appear when they become available.
