Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: MARCH 16, 2026

Welcome to Multipost. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our multi-posting services, we collect:

  • Account Information: Your email and name via Firebase Authentication.
  • Connected Accounts: Secure tokens required to post to your YouTube and TikTok accounts.
  • Content Data: Media and text you upload for the purpose of social media distribution.

2. Third-Party API Disclosure (TikTok & Google)

Multipost uses TikTok and Google API Services to enable video distribution. By using Multipost, you also agree to be bound by:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Data Deletion & Your Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Instant Deletion: You can use the "Delete Account" button in your Profile settings to permanently erase your account and all associated tokens immediately.
  • Manual Removal: You may contact support@multipostapp.co.uk to request data removal.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke Multipost's access to your social accounts at any time through your Google or TikTok security settings.

4. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to facilitate the organization and posting of content to your connected accounts. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
