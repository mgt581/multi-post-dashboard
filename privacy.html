Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Multipost ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how your information is handled. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal data when you use our web application located at multipostapp.co.uk.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users. This includes:

  • Account Information: When you sign up via Google or other methods, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Content Data: We process the posts, images, and captions you upload to the service for the purpose of multi-platform distribution.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with our service, such as log files and device information.

3. Google User Data Policy

Our application uses Google OAuth services. If you choose to link your Google account:

  • Access: We access your basic profile info and any specific permissions you grant (e.g., YouTube API access) solely to perform the requested actions (e.g., posting a video).
  • Storage: We do not store your Google password. Access tokens are stored securely and encrypted.
  • Limited Use: Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Multipost service.
  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Distribute your content to the platforms you select.
  • Communicate with you regarding updates or support.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist with hosting, analytics, or payment processing (under strict confidentiality agreements).
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request the deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at support@multipostapp.co.uk.
