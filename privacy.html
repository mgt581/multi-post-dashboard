Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: APRIL 7, 2026

Multipost is operated by Bryant Group Holdings, trading as Bryant Digital Solutions. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our multi-posting services, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Your name and email via Firebase Authentication.
  • Connected Accounts: Secure access tokens required to post to platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, and other supported services.
  • Content Data: Media and text you upload for publishing.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics and logs to improve performance and security.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to operate and improve the Multipost platform, including:

  • Publishing content to your connected accounts
  • Managing your account and authentication
  • Improving platform performance and security

We do not sell your personal data.

Facebook Data Deletion

To request deletion of Facebook data linked to your Multipost workspace, email support@multipostapp.co.uk. We will remove the linked Facebook account, Page connection, and stored Facebook access tokens from our systems. Facebook can also send an authenticated deletion request directly to our callback at https://multipostapp.co.uk/api/auth/facebook/data-deletion.

3. Data Processors & Infrastructure

We use trusted third-party services to operate our platform, including:

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database services)
  • Cloudflare (hosting, APIs, and security infrastructure)

These providers process data solely on behalf of Multipost and in accordance with our instructions. They do not use your data for their own purposes.

4. Third-Party API Disclosure

Multipost integrates with third-party platforms such as TikTok and Google (YouTube). All third-party platform data is accessed only through explicit user consent via secure OAuth 2.0 authorization flows. By using Multipost, you agree to their respective policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

4A. Google User Data Usage & Disclosure

Multipost requests access to your Google account solely to enable YouTube publishing functionality. Specifically, we access:

  • Basic profile information (name, email address) — used only for account identification and authentication.
  • YouTube account access — used only to upload and publish content to your YouTube channel on your behalf.

Google user data obtained through OAuth is used exclusively for the core functionality of Multipost (authentication and content publishing). We do not:

  • Sell or transfer Google user data to any third parties.
  • Use Google user data for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • Use Google user data for any purpose unrelated to providing the Multipost service.

Your Google user data is processed only on behalf of Multipost by the following infrastructure providers, solely as necessary to operate the platform:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database services.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, API routing, and security infrastructure.

These providers do not use your Google user data for their own purposes. Multipost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

You may revoke Multipost's access to your Google account at any time by visiting: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

5. Data Deletion & Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Instant Deletion: Use the in-app delete account feature to permanently remove your data.
  • Manual Requests: Contact support@multipostapp.co.uk for deletion requests.
  • Revoke Access: You can revoke permissions via your connected platform settings at any time.

5A. Account Deletion Requests (Google Play Requirement)

If you would like your Multipost account and associated personal data deleted, you can request deletion at any time. We provide multiple options to make deletion simple and accessible.

  • In-app deletion: If available, use the in-app delete account feature to permanently remove your account and associated data.
  • Email deletion request: Send an email to support@multipostapp.co.uk with the subject line "Delete My Account" from the email address linked to your account.
  • What we may ask for: To prevent unauthorized deletion, we may ask you to confirm ownership of the account (for example, by replying from your registered email).
  • Deletion timeframe: We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law or for security/fraud prevention.
  • What gets deleted: Your authentication profile, connected account tokens (where applicable), and other personal data associated with your account will be removed. Some limited records (e.g., security logs) may be retained for a short period where necessary for legitimate interests, legal compliance, or fraud prevention.

6. Legal Basis, Retention & International Transfers (UK/EU)

This section applies where UK/EU data protection laws apply:

  • Legal basis: We process your data to provide the service (contract) and to secure/improve the platform (legitimate interests).
  • Retention: We keep account and connected account data until you delete your account or disconnect a platform. Security logs may be retained for up to 30 days for fraud prevention and troubleshooting.
  • International transfers: Our providers may process data in the UK/EEA and other countries where they operate. We rely on appropriate safeguards where required.
  • Your rights: You can request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your data. UK users may complain to the ICO.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
