Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 16, 2026

At Multipost (multipostapp.co.uk), we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our multi-platform scheduling and AI optimization services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, Multipost collects the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address provided via Firebase Authentication.
  • Connected Social Accounts: When you link YouTube or TikTok, we receive authorization tokens (OAuth) that allow us to post on your behalf.
  • Media Content: Video files and metadata (titles/descriptions) you upload to our platform for distribution.
  • AI Interaction Data: The prompts you provide to our Llama-3 AI engine to generate SEO metadata.
Data Security: We do not store your social media passwords. All connections are handled via secure official API tokens which you can revoke at any time from your platform's security settings.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely to operate Multipost, including:

  • Facilitating the upload of videos to your connected YouTube and TikTok channels.
  • Using AI to generate viral-optimized titles and descriptions based on your inputs.
  • Maintaining your workspace folders and account preferences.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

Multipost interacts with the following third-party services:

  • Google/YouTube API: Used to upload videos. By using this service, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok API: Used for content distribution. You can review the TikTok Privacy Policy for more details.
  • Cloudflare: We use Cloudflare Workers to process your requests securely at the edge.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your data within Multipost:

  • Revocation: You can disconnect your social media accounts at any time, which immediately deletes the associated access tokens from our database.
  • Deletion: You may request full account deletion by contacting support. This will remove all your stored folders, metadata, and personal information.
