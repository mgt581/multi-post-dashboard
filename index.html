How Multipost works

Create a workspace for a brand, connect supported social platforms using their official authorisation flow, prepare content and publish to the selected account.

1. Create a workspace

Keep each brand or client organised in its own workspace.

2. Connect platforms

Authorise TikTok, Facebook or YouTube only when you choose to connect that platform.

3. Prepare content

Upload media and generate platform-specific titles, descriptions, captions and hashtags.

4. Publish

Review the content and send it to the connected social account using the platform integration.
