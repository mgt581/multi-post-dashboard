How Multipost works

One straightforward flow for every account you manage—without rewriting the same post or hunting for the right client login.

1. Organise every brand

Create a workspace for each client, business or personal channel and keep its connected accounts together.

2. Upload, then let AI help

Add a video thumbnail or image. Multipost can generate SEO-aware titles, descriptions, captions and hashtags—even when you do not know what to write.

3. Get copy for each platform

Review and edit distinct YouTube, TikTok and Facebook copy instead of sending the same generic wording everywhere.

4. Publish with confidence

Send media to the account stored in that workspace, with a clear fallback route whenever a platform needs manual completion.

Useful whether you run one account or one hundred

Multipost starts simple for a single creator, then stays organised as you add businesses and clients.

Creators

Turn an image or video idea into ready-to-edit copy and avoid uploading the same content three times.

Local businesses

Save website links, phone numbers and email addresses once, then automatically include them in relevant Facebook image captions.

Agencies & freelancers

Keep client accounts in separate workspaces, switch between brands quickly and choose a plan that fits the number of accounts you manage.
